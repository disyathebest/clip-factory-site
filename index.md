# Clip Factory

Clip Factory is a private tool used by two people, Andrew and Disya, to edit short vertical clips from livestreams and post them to our own **Clipped Daily** accounts on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram.

- We only clip streamers who have given permission.
- Every post credits the streamer by name, with a link to their channel.
- Clip Factory posts only to accounts we own and have connected ourselves. It is not offered to the public, and nobody else can sign up for it or sign in to it.
- Account access tokens stay on our own computers. Clip Factory collects no data about viewers.

Clip Factory uses YouTube API Services and follows the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Its use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.
