# Clip Factory Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 28, 2026_

Clip Factory is a private tool used by two people, Andrew and Disya ("we", "us"), to edit short clips from livestreams and post them to our own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts. We only clip streamers who have given permission, and every post credits the streamer with a link to their channel.

Clip Factory is not offered to the public. Nobody else can sign up for it or sign in to it.

## What information we use

**Information about our own accounts.** When we connect our own YouTube, TikTok or Instagram account to Clip Factory, the platform gives us an access token. Clip Factory uses that token only to upload our videos and their titles, descriptions and tags to our own account, and to read back the link and status of each post.

**Public stream videos.** To make clips, Clip Factory downloads publicly available stream recordings from streamers who allow clipping. It does not collect personal information about the people in those streams beyond what is in the public video itself.

**Nothing from viewers.** Clip Factory does not collect, store or process any information about people who watch, like, comment on or follow our posts. It has no analytics, ads, tracking cookies or user sign-ups.

## Where it is stored

Access tokens, videos and settings are stored only on our own computers. They are not sent to any server of ours, sold, rented or shared with anyone.

The only places Clip Factory sends data are the platforms we post to (YouTube, TikTok and Instagram), and only to upload our own posts. Those platforms handle the data under their own policies:

- YouTube: [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- TikTok: [TikTok Privacy Policy](https://www.tiktok.com/legal/privacy-policy)
- Instagram / Meta: [Meta Privacy Policy](https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy)

Clip Factory uses YouTube API Services. By using it to post to YouTube, we agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.

Clip Factory's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## How long we keep it

Access tokens are kept until we disconnect the account or revoke access. Videos and clip files are deleted from our computers when we no longer need them.

## How to revoke access or delete data

You can remove Clip Factory's access to an account at any time:

- Google / YouTube: [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions), select Clip Factory, then **Remove access**
- TikTok: in the TikTok app, **Profile → ☰ → Settings and privacy → Security & permissions → Apps and services permissions**, then remove Clip Factory
- Instagram / Facebook: **Settings → Apps and websites** (Instagram) or **Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations** (Facebook), then remove Clip Factory

Revoking access stops Clip Factory from using the account right away. To ask us to delete any data related to you, including if you are a streamer whose clips we posted and you want them taken down, email us at the address below. We will reply and delete it within 30 days.

## Children

Clip Factory is not meant for, and does not collect information from, anyone under 13.
